## Synthesis

Posts benefit from a coherent story or material transition that remains clear in a mobile feed. Avatars need one dominant face and broad visual marks. The same attractive full-frame artwork may fail as a profile picture; the new linocut concept therefore has independent composition rather than a crop of the railway print. The paper-framed avatar keeps the face photographic, unlike the retained paper-relief post. Rain is contained outside facial landmarks. Clay relief is experimental and should be rejected if material shadow erases identity.

No exact competitor title, prompt, example asset or layout is proposed for reuse. Originality here is an editorial comparison with inspected references and current PicMagic content, not a claim to have searched every image ever published. New media has not been generated.
