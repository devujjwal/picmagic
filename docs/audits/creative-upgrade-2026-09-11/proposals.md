## P01. The River Remembers

**Slug:** `the-river-remembers` · **Mechanism:** photo-illustration · **Score:** 5/5 · **Ratio:** 4:5 · **Batch rank:** 1

**Detail destination:** `/ai-prompts/travel/the-river-remembers`. Proposed only; not a live route.

**Collection/category destinations:** `/collections/travel`, `/collections/creative-transformations`

**Visual narrative:** A traveller rests an open journal on a ghat step. One river edge flows out of its page as translucent ink, becoming a real river behind the person; the face, hand and nearest stone remain photographic. The transition follows a curved shoreline, never a straight split.

**Reference requirements:** One clear adult portrait; optional owned river/location photograph. No requirement to upload handwriting or private journal pages.

**Preserve:** One recognizable face and actual proportions; keep the photographic face outside the painted region.

**Transform:** Environment edges and blank journal artwork into flowing ink; adapt practical clothing and a quiet glance.

**Fictional preview persona and scene:** One fictional Indian woman about 42, deep-brown complexion, short wavy hair, ordinary build, in an indigo cotton shirt; copper river light, cream paper and one indigo ink channel. Three-quarter framing, face large and unobscured.

**Why AI / user benefit:** Turns a personal travel photograph into a physical-looking memory artwork that requires compositing, illustration and perspective matching.

**Originality:** Continuous river-led transition and a single face, unlike the references' stacked repeated-scene pages; no copied captions.

**Execution risks:** Ink must not cross facial landmarks or imply the person is dissolving; avoid tiny journal detail as the only hook.

**Reuse and overlap:** Could develop the generic island or Paris travel memory, but the new river narrative warrants its own detail record; no existing image proves the effect.

**Reel potential:** A restrained ink-edge reveal could work after a still succeeds; not selected for initial motion production.

**Evidence:** AP01, CG03, YM07 in [research log](research.json); [local audit](prompt-audit.json).
