## Asset decision

The 11 shared images are the seven explicitly listed photo/reel pairs plus four dating pairs. They remain shared concept art, not presented as rendered video. Each has a different output workflow, distinct URL, self-canonical and an explicit link to the other medium. Google does not require every page to own an exclusive image. We are not fabricating new posters or duplicating files to imply uniqueness. Future genuine motion previews may replace stills only after media review. No asset was deleted, remapped or regenerated in this SEO batch.

## Indexing policy

No URL is removed solely on a lexical score; no automatic canonicalisation or mass noindex is applied. Sparse/unreviewed hubs remain noindex. New permutation pages are not added. Before expanding the library, compare search intent, example visual, useful instructions and existing destinations. Investigate future overlap notices during editorial review; reject exact duplicate descriptions in release validation. The decision to keep these pages is editorial judgment, not a guarantee that Google will index or rank them.
